## 11. FAQs

Title: `faqs`

| #   | Question                                         |
|-----|--------------------------------------------------|
| /01 | what happens when i join?                        |
| /02 | are sessions recorded?                           |
| /03 | what happens during sessions?                    |
| /04 | do i need to know how to code?                   |
| /05 | what if i don't have an idea?                    |
| /06 | who runs code & coffee?                          |

**Answers**

* **/01 — what happens when i join?**
  > you join a session and become part of the community. you start learning, building, and interacting with others.

* **/02 — are sessions recorded?**
  > some sessions are recorded. but being present gives you the full experience.

* **/03 — what happens during sessions?**
  > coding, workshops, discussions, demos, and guest sessions. each session is different, but the focus stays the same: learn and build.

* **/04 — do i need to know how to code?**
  > no. beginners are welcome. you just need interest and consistency.

* **/05 — what if i don't have an idea?**
  > that's fine. you'll discover ideas through sessions, discussions, and problems around you.

* **/06 — who runs code & coffee?**
  > it's student-led and community-driven, with support from builders, developers, and creators.
